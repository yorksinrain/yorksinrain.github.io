GRADUATE WORK

KEIO KMD · EMBODIED MEDIA LAB · 2026

Work from my first year at Keio, much of it still in progress. Each project is shown as a short note for now.

SPATIAL INTERACTION

Pointy

A single laser dot that lets a room point back. I design how people and space talk through it: a space that remembers where things were left, confirms what you meant when a gesture is ambiguous, and lets you drag the dot by hand.

Role
Interaction and scenario design; the device is developed by the lab team
Status
Virtual demo Nov. 2026 · first in-room prototype Dec. 2026
HARDWARE · BMI PERFORMANCE

Muto-box

A modular mounting system for a brain–machine interface performance rig. It carries the rig's compute, motor-control and audio units on the back of the performer's wheelchair, so live BMI performances can travel with the performer, and any cable can be re-plugged mid-show.

Role
Layout, 3D modeling, fabrication and on-chair testing; the BMI system is developed by the lab team
Status
v1.0 in use · Nov. 2026
TANGIBLE INTERACTION · IVRC 2026

Stacking Light

An exploration experience in a dark room where physical blocks decide what you can see. Placing blocks on a 3 × 3 sensing base opens a path; stacking them lights walls and heights, and a wrist band hums near hidden clues.

Team
Three members, Embodied Media Lab
Status
IVRC 2026 accepted project · shown at VRSJ 2026
RESEARCH · BRAND

Enlight

A browser companion that makes manipulative interface design visible, then explains it in plain words. Its character is a sphere whose only face is a cross, so a turn or a nod is enough to say what it noticed.

Role
User research, brand identity and pitch; the extension was coded by a teammate
Context
Build With Claude Hackathon, Oct. 2026

Generative AI: the extension's detection and explanations are built on Claude (Anthropic).

BRAND · TEAM

三日所 KEPTO

Role
Co-founder
Status
Ongoing since 2026
